## Doctrine from the human's T1–106 (this is what beat Fable's Game 3 on the same map)
1. **Districts are why humans win.** Fable G3 froze at 25 districts across 15 cities from T111 to T150 and lost T164. The human had 57 complete at T101. Every city with a free slot (1 per 3 pop) has a district at the top of its queue unless under active siege. Never let a queue sit empty.
2. **Chops fund everything.** Magnus (Groundbreaker +50%) sits in the city that is about to build a settler/district/wonder; a builder chops forest/jungle/marsh INTO that item the same turn. A chop = 40–90 production. Fable G3 made zero strategic chops. Use `run_lua` if no tool exposes harvest: `UnitManager.RequestOperation(unit, UnitOperationTypes.HARVEST_RESOURCE / REMOVE_FEATURE ...)` — the civ6-mcp lua/ dir has the snippets.
3. **Qin builder charges on wonders**: builder ON the wonder tile with moves → `UnitManager.RequestCommand(unit, UnitCommandTypes.WONDER_PRODUCTION)`. One charge/turn/builder. This is how Stonehenge, Petra, Mausoleum, Forbidden City were taken at Deity. Great Engineers (Mausoleum = +1 charge, Invention card) are the Renaissance wonder engine — never leave one idle.
4. **Plan then execute.** The pins above are the plan. Build on pinned tiles. Adjacency was computed by the human; trust it.
5. **Faith → Theocracy purchases.** Faith buys Crouching Tigers / units for defense and, with Monumentality, builders/settlers.
6. **Leonardo da Vinci** → +culture per Workshop: Workshop in every Industrial Zone city.
7. **The western chain** (Beijing, Wuhan, Shenyang, Shanghai, Xuez) is one city wide and holds Scythia north. Loyalty is fine while no foreign city sits within 9 tiles; keep governors/Monuments there. Encampments at Beijing and Shenyang, not walls everywhere.
8. **Science is the gap** — Germany has 2× science with fewer districts because their districts have BUILDINGS. Libraries → Universities in every Campus before more Campuses. Germany wins on science if unchecked (that is exactly how G3 ended).
9. **Military floor**: never fewer than ~6 real units; Deity AIs declare on the weakest neighbor. Germany's strength fell from 950 to 378 around T100 — they are rebuilding; you have a window.
10. Era score: track distance to the Golden threshold every 5 turns and buy the last points deliberately (the human missed a Golden Age by ONE point at T~40).
