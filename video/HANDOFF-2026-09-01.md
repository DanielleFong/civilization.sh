## Turn-loop fixes learned T111–113
- run_lua only returns `print()` output, not `return` values.
- After Lua ACTION_ENDTURN, if turn counter doesn't advance: check get_pending_diplomacy → respond_to_diplomacy(id,"positive") then respond_to_diplomacy(id,"exit") (SESSION_CLOSED). Also WC sessions need MCP end_turn twice (queue_wc_votes between).
- Map pins readable: PlayerConfigurations[local]:GetMapPins() → pin:GetName()/GetHexX()/GetHexY()/GetIconName(). Danielle uses pins as directives (T112: Aqueduct@87,45, Dam@86,45 for Jiaodong).
- City id pattern: n*65536 + (n-1) for the n-th founded city.
- Barb naval units capture embarked/coastal civilians: never put a civilian on a water tile with a barb ship within 6 tiles (lost settler T106, builder T110).
- Idle-city finder (gamecore ctx): for _,c in Players[<human>]:GetCities():Members() do print(c:GetID(), Locale.Lookup(c:GetName()), c:GetBuildQueue():CurrentlyBuilding()) end  → "NONE" marks the city blocking end-turn. (CurrentlyBuilding is nil in ingame ctx.)
